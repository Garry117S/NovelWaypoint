Journey to the Center of the Earth.

Jules Verne

Recommended for fans of: Minecraft

Genre: Adventure / Science fiction / Classic fiction

First published: 1864

About the book.

When Professor Otto Lidenbrock discovers a mysterious message hidden inside an old manuscript, he becomes convinced that it contains the instructions to reach the centre of the Earth.

Along with his nephew Axel and their Icelandic guide Hans, he sets out on an extraordinary expedition beneath the surface of the planet.

Their journey takes them through underground caverns, enormous chambers, ancient rock formations and strange subterranean environments. Along the way they encounter unexpected dangers and discoveries that challenge everything they thought they knew about the world beneath their feet.

What to expect.

Journey to the Center of the Earth is a classic adventure built around exploration and discovery. The characters constantly move deeper into an unknown environment, never knowing what they might find next.

If you enjoy Minecraft's caves, underground exploration, rare discoveries and the feeling that there is an entire world hidden beneath the surface, this is an especially natural recommendation.

Expect mysterious tunnels, enormous underground spaces, dangerous creatures, scientific discoveries and the excitement of exploring somewhere that nobody else has mapped.
