Neuromancer

William Gibson

Recommended for fans of: Cyberpunk 2077

Genre: Cyberpunk / science fiction

First published: 1984

About the book

Case is a washed-up computer hacker living on the edge of society in a future dominated by technology, powerful corporations and artificial intelligence.

Given one last chance to return to the world of cyberspace, Case is recruited for a dangerous job involving a mysterious employer, a highly skilled team and a mission that could have consequences far beyond anything he expected.

As the mission unfolds, Case finds himself caught between human ambition, artificial intelligence and forces operating far beyond his understanding.

What to expect

If you enjoyed Cyberpunk 2077 for its neon-soaked cities, hackers, corporations, cybernetic technology and uneasy relationship between humanity and machines, Neuromancer is one of the most important books to read.

Expect cyberspace, hacking, artificial intelligence, corporate power, cybernetic enhancement, dangerous technology and a world where the boundary between human beings and machines is becoming increasingly blurred.
