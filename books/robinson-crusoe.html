Robinson Crusoe

Daniel Defoe

Recommended for fans of: Minecraft

Genre: Survival adventure / classic fiction

First published: 1719

About the book

After being shipwrecked on a remote island, Robinson Crusoe must learn how to survive with very limited supplies and no immediate hope of rescue.

He builds shelter, searches for food, creates tools and gradually transforms the island into a place he can call home. Every improvement depends on patience, planning and making the most of the resources available to him.

Robinson Crusoe is one of the classic survival stories. Its appeal comes from watching a character gather materials, solve practical problems and build a life from almost nothing — making it a natural recommendation for Minecraft players who enjoy exploration and self-sufficiency.

What to expect

  • Survival on a remote island
  • Gathering and managing limited resources
  • Building shelter and useful tools
  • Exploration and discovery
  • Problem-solving and self-sufficiency
  • Isolation and resilience
  • A foundational survival adventure
