The Last Kids on Earth

Max Brallier

Recommended for fans of: Minecraft

Genre: Children's fiction / post-apocalyptic adventure

First published: 2015

About the book

Jack Sullivan is having the best day of his life. The world has ended, monsters have taken over, and he finally gets to live in his very own treehouse fortress.

With a group of friends, homemade weapons and plenty of determination, Jack sets out to survive the apocalypse while battling zombies and enormous monsters. What begins as a seemingly hopeless situation quickly becomes an adventure filled with friendship, exploration, creativity and plenty of chaos.

What to expect

If you enjoy the feeling of starting with very little and gradually building up a safe base, gathering useful supplies and preparing for whatever might appear next, this is a great match for Minecraft players.

Expect monsters, exploration, survival, improvised weapons, friendship and the satisfaction of turning a dangerous world into somewhere you can call home.
