Fantasy recommendation

The Name of the Wind

Patrick Rothfuss

The Kingkiller Chronicle — Book 1

A gifted musician, a mysterious past and a dangerous journey into the world of magic, legends and ancient secrets.

View on Amazon

About the book

When Kvothe, a gifted young musician and storyteller, is left orphaned, he must survive through hardship, poverty and danger while pursuing the mysteries of magic and the truth behind his family's tragedy.

His journey eventually leads him to a legendary university, where he studies arcane knowledge, makes powerful friends and enemies, and begins uncovering secrets that could change his life forever.

The Name of the Wind is an immersive fantasy adventure filled with magic, ancient mysteries, music, dangerous creatures and a richly developed world. Its combination of exploration, powerful characters, hidden lore and personal ambition makes it a strong choice for readers who enjoy becoming absorbed in a deep fantasy world.
