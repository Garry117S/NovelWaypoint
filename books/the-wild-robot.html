Minecraft recommendation

The Wild Robot

Peter Brown

Recommended for fans of: Minecraft

Genre: Science fiction / survival adventure

First published: 2016

About the book

When a robot named Roz is washed ashore on a remote, uninhabited island, she must learn how to survive in a world she was never designed to understand.

At first, Roz relies on observation and experimentation. She studies the animals, learns the island’s rhythms and adapts to the environment around her. Gradually, she begins to build relationships and create a home of her own.

The Wild Robot is a thoughtful adventure about survival, adaptation and learning how to belong. Its combination of exploration, resourcefulness, nature and building a life from scratch makes it a great choice for Minecraft players.

What to expect

  • Survival on a remote island
  • Exploration and discovery
  • Learning from the natural environment
  • Building a home and adapting to circumstances
  • Animals and the natural world
  • Friendship, cooperation and belonging
  • A warm and imaginative adventure
