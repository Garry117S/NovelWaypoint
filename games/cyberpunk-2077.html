Books for Cyberpunk 2077 players.

Cyberpunk 2077 takes you into a world of towering corporations, advanced technology, artificial intelligence, cybernetic bodies and people trying to survive in a city where almost everything has a price.

But cyberpunk isn't just about neon lights and futuristic technology. At its heart are stories about power, freedom, identity, inequality, rebellion and what it means to remain human in a world where technology is changing everything.

That's what we've used to choose the books below.
