Minecraft.

Minecraft is a game about exploration, survival and creativity. You enter a huge block-based world where you can gather resources, build almost anything you can imagine, explore underground caves and distant landscapes, and face dangerous creatures after dark.

There isn't one way to play Minecraft. Some players love the challenge of surviving with limited resources, while others enjoy building enormous creations, exploring the world, discovering secrets or simply seeing what lies beyond the horizon.

That's what we've used to choose the books below.

Whether you've played Minecraft for hundreds of hours or have never picked up the game, find the part of Minecraft that sounds most appealing to you and discover your next book.

Hatchet

Gary Paulsen

After a plane crash leaves thirteen-year-old Brian Robeson stranded in the Canadian wilderness, he must learn to survive with little more than the clothes he is wearing and a single hatchet.

Brian has to find food, build shelter and learn how to use the natural world around him. Every discovery and every mistake matters as he gradually develops the skills he needs to stay alive.

A gripping survival story about resourcefulness, independence and learning through experience.

After being shipwrecked on a remote island, Robinson Crusoe must learn how to survive with very limited supplies and no immediate hope of rescue.
