Minecraft.

Find your next adventure beyond the blocky world.

Books for Minecraft players.

Minecraft is a game about exploration, survival and creativity. You enter a huge block-based world where you can gather resources, build almost anything you can imagine, explore underground caves and distant landscapes, and face dangerous creatures after dark.

There isn't one way to play Minecraft. Some players love the challenge of surviving with limited resources, while others enjoy building enormous creations, exploring the world, discovering secrets or simply seeing what lies beyond the horizon.

That's what we've used to choose the books below.

Find the part of Minecraft you enjoy most and discover a story that captures some of that same feeling.
