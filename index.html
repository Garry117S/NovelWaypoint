Minecraft.

Minecraft is a game about exploration, survival and creativity. You enter a huge block-based world where you can gather resources, build almost anything you can imagine, explore underground caves and distant landscapes, and face dangerous creatures after dark.

Whether you love surviving with limited resources, building enormous creations, exploring hidden places or simply seeing what lies beyond the horizon, there are plenty of books that capture those same feelings.

We've grouped the recommendations by the parts of Minecraft you enjoy most, making it easier to find a story that feels familiar while taking you somewhere completely new.

Cyberpunk 2077.

Night City is a world of neon lights, powerful corporations, advanced technology and people trying to survive in a future where humanity and machines are becoming increasingly difficult to separate.

If you enjoy futuristic cities, cybernetic technology, artificial intelligence, corporate power, rebellion, questions of identity and the darker side of progress, there are plenty of books that explore similar ideas.

From classic cyberpunk that helped define the genre to dystopian stories about technology, society and survival, these recommendations are for anyone looking for their next journey beyond Night City.
